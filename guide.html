Oregon LiDAR Map

A practical guide to terrain, public land ownership, land-survey sections, fire activity, weather stations, waterbodies, GPS positioning, and track files.

Use the layer menu in the map’s upper-right corner to turn overlays on and off. You can combine useful layers, change the base map, and click map features to view their available details.

Base maps and terrain

Esri Street Map

Roads and place references.

Use this as a general-purpose background for finding roads, communities, and geographic context. Max zoom level is 19.

OpenTopoMap

Terrain and topographic context.

Use this to help interpret the surrounding terrain and landscape in a topography format. Max zoom is 17.

Esri Aerial Imagery

Satellite and aerial-style imagery.

Satellite imagery when you want to compare mapped features against visible ground conditions, max zoom is 19.

DOGAMI LiDAR

Terrain hillshade overlay.

LiDAR hillshade emphasizes landform and terrain shape. Use the LiDaR opacity slider to adjust its visibility over the selected base map.
A hillshade is a shaded terrain visualization; it is not the same thing as a survey-grade elevation measurement. This data is collected from airborne laser range finding equipment measuring differences in ground elevation in minuscule detail.

Land-survey and natural features

PLSS Sections

Public Land Survey System overlay.

Displays section boundaries to help identify survey-section locations. Use the underlying map and available survey records for additional context; the overlay is not a substitute for a legal survey.

Public Land Ownership

Oregon Department of Forestry ownership overlay.

Displays public land ownership information from the Oregon Department of Forestry map service. Use it to compare mapped ownership areas with roads, terrain, and PLSS sections. Coverage and categories depend on the source data. This layer is a general reference, not a legal property boundary or proof of public access.

Lake/Pond ¼ Acre+

USGS mapped waterbodies.

The layer displays qualifying mapped lakes and ponds in Jackson and Josephine counties. Waterbody polygons load for the current map view; click a polygon for the attributes provided.

Fire and weather overlays

NIFC Fires (24h)

Recent incident locations across Oregon from the NIFC/IRWIN feed.

Red: Wildfire (WF)
Green: Prescribed fire (RX)
Orange: Incident complex (CX)

Click a marker for the incident name, type, discovery date/time, county, and protecting agency when reported. The layer refreshes periodically; feed information may be incomplete or delayed.

SWO Current

Current fire perimeter styling

Shows current NIFC perimeter features filtered to Jackson and Josephine counties. Click a perimeter for its reported name, county, and mapped acreage when available.

The outline and fill indicate the mapped perimeter, not necessarily the exact location of active flames or the current evacuation boundary.

SWO Historical

Historical perimeter styling

Displays historical fire perimeters from 2020 onward for Jackson and Josephine counties, subject to the records returned by the service.

Click a polygon to view available incident information, discovery and perimeter dates, acreage, containment information, and coordinates.

RAWS WX Stations

Active RAWS weather stations throughout Oregon.

Blue station markers include a temperature label when data is available. Click a station for its reported temperature, humidity, wind speed and direction, peak wind, rain accumulation, elevation, observation time, and any available detailed-weather link.
Weather values depend on the station's latest returned observation. Missing or stale readings may occur.

GPS and track tools

◎ GPS / GPS Track

Your device's current GPS position
Recorded GPS track
  • Tap ◎ GPS to request device location access.
  • The map centers on the first reported position.
  • The coordinate display uses degrees and decimal minutes (DDM); west longitudes are negative.
  • The blue circle represents reported GPS accuracy.
  • GPS Track can be switched on or off in the layer menu. Turning its display off does not itself stop recording.
  • Tap CLEAR TRACK to erase the recorded GPS lines.
GPS requires a supported browser, device location services, permission, and a secure HTTPS page. Keep location sharing enabled only when you want to use it.

OPEN GPX / CLEAR GPX

Import tracks and routes from GPX files stored on your device.

  • Tap OPEN GPX and select one or more GPX files.
  • Each imported file gets its own checkbox in the layer menu.
  • Track and route lines are drawn separately where the file contains separate segments.
  • Named waypoints and start/end markers may also be displayed.
  • The map zooms to the imported track's extent.
  • Tap CLEAR GPX to remove all imported GPX layers from the map and layer menu.
GPX files are read locally in your browser by this map code; the import function does not upload them to the map server.

Coordinate display

Click Map for Coordinates: click a point on the map to display its latitude and longitude in degrees and decimal minutes.

Example format: 42° 26.208'   -123° 08.400'

Negative longitude values indicate west. The coordinate display is intended as a convenient location reference; confirm coordinates and boundaries against authoritative data when precision matters.
